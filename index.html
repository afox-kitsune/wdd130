Arizona

Arizona is in the Southwestern region of the United States. It ranks in the top 15 in both size and population of states in the United states. Arizona has a desert climate, in the winter, temperatures are mild averaging 75 degrees farenheit while summer is considered a dry heat with daytime temperatures averaging triple digits. Arizona has a diverse plant life but are most well known of their cactus. They also have several national parks including Saguaro and Pretrified Forest however the most famous and one of the severn natural world wonders is the Grand Canyon.
